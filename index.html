Outdo
Beat friends. Outdo yourself.

Fitness competition without the ceiling

Outdo turns your Apple Watch Activity rings into a competition with your friends — scored against real targets, with no caps on effort and nowhere to hide a soft goal.

How it works

  • No scoring caps. Doubling your Move goal pays double. Effort keeps counting instead of stopping at 100%.
  • Everyone sees everything. Exact ring values, exact goals, exact points. You can't win a competition by setting yourself an easy target — the group sets the target, not the individual.
  • Nudge a friend whose numbers look stale and their app syncs fresh data on the spot.
  • Competitions that keep going in repeating rounds, with every finished round's result kept.
Privacy Policy What's read from Apple Health, what your competitors can see, and how to delete it all. Support Flat rings, stale scores, rounds, invites, notifications and account deletion.
