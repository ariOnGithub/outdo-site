Joining by hand

If the button above doesn't take you straight in, the code works anywhere: open Outdo, go to the Arena tab, tap Join, and enter it.

What you'll need

  • An iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
  • An Apple Watch for full scoring — Outdo reads your Move, Exercise and Stand rings. Without one you can still follow a competition and see everyone's numbers, but you won't appear on the leaderboard.
  • A few seconds to sign in with Apple and pick a username.
